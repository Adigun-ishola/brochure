Pyne Awards Africa 2026 brings together leaders, institutions and professionals from across Africa's tourism and hospitality ecosystem for three days of leadership dialogue, business exchange, recognition, culture and destination experience in Maputo, Mozambique.

From the ELEVATE Women Leadership Breakfast and continental business meetings to the 6th Pyne Awards Africa Gala and a curated experience of Maputo Bay, the programme creates a platform for meaningful connections while celebrating the people, organisations and destinations shaping the future of African tourism.

24 SEPTEMBER 2026

LEADERSHIP • WELCOME • CONNECTIONS

Pyne Awards Africa opens with a day dedicated to leadership, connection and a warm introduction to Mozambique.

Arrival, Registration & Welcome

Arrival and registration of guests ahead of ELEVATE, with an opportunity for informal networking over welcome refreshments.

08:00am – 09:00am Polana Serena Hotel

ELEVATE – Pyne
Women Leadership Breakfast

A high-level gathering of influential women leaders from tourism, hospitality, aviation, government, investment and business, convened under the theme “Leading the Future: Women Driving Africa's Tourism Transformation”. The breakfast will create space for leadership dialogue, shared experience and connections across markets and sectors.

  • Opening and welcome.
  • High-level address.
  • Moderated leadership conversation.
  • Perspectives from women leaders across Africa.
  • Breakfast and executive networking.
  • Closing reflections and official photograph.

Maputo Afternoon
and Bilateral Meetings

An open afternoon for delegates to settle into the city, hold bilateral and business meetings, or discover selected cultural, culinary and lifestyle experiences across Maputo.

The official welcome reception for Pyne Awards Africa 2026, bringing together international delegates, nominees, partners and invited guests against the backdrop of Maputo Bay. The evening combines Mozambican hospitality, culture and informal networking in a relaxed setting.

  • Red carpet arrivals and official photography.
  • Welcome reception.
  • Opening remarks and partner acknowledgements.
  • Mozambique destination moment.
